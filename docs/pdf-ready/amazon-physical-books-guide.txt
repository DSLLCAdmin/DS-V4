Amazon Seller Central - Adding Physical Books (NOT E-books)




🚨 CRITICAL ISSUE IDENTIFIED


You're trying to add e-book ASIN B0FDH86NJJ to your physical inventory catalog. This won't work because:  • E-books = Digital products managed through KDP  • Physical books = Inventory products managed through Seller Central  • FBA fulfillment only works with physical products



✅ SOLUTION: Add Physical Paperback Versions




Step 1: Find Your Physical Book ASINs
  1. Go to Amazon.com (not Seller Central)  2. Search for each book + "paperback":  • "First & Light paperback"  • "Risque & Safety paperback"  • "Mercury & Memory paperback"  • "DarkStreets paperback"  • "The Fall paperback"  • "The Rise paperback"  • "The End paperback"  • "The Beginning paperback"  3. Look for the physical book listing (not the e-book)  4. Copy the ASIN from the URL or product details  5. Note: The paperback ASIN will be DIFFERENT from B0FDH86NJJ



Step 2: Add Physical Books to Seller Central
  1. Go to Seller Central → Catalog → Add Products  2. Search for the paperback ASIN (not the e-book ASIN)  3. Click "Sell this product" if it exists  4. Or create new listing if it doesn't exist



Step 3: Configure for FBA
  1. Set fulfillment method to "Amazon FBA"  2. Add inventory (if you have physical stock)  3. Set pricing and shipping settings



📚 EXAMPLE:
  • ❌ WRONG: B0FDH86NJJ (e-book - can't be fulfilled)  • ✅ RIGHT: [Find paperback ASIN] (physical - can be fulfilled)



🔗 HELPFUL LINKS:
  • Amazon.com search: https://amazon.com  • KDP Dashboard: https://kdp.amazon.com  • Seller Central: https://sellercentral.amazon.com



⚠️ IMPORTANT NOTES:
  • E-books are managed through KDP (not Seller Central)  • Physical books are managed through Seller Central  • FBA fulfillment only works with physical products  • You need paperback ASINs (not e-book ASINs)
